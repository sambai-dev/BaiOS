# Make every Workbench proof surface explicitly truthful

Status: **Completed — 2026-08-30.** The Systems, Brief, and Agent reframing plus corresponding registry/API copy are part of the 2026-08-30 release.

Written against: 439ff49866483eed6421c4d52ed12d0e88ad35e6

## Evidence chain

- Surface: `/` → `app/page.tsx` → `app/components/PortfolioShell.tsx` → `app/components/WorkbenchOS.tsx` → `app/components/WorkbenchOSV3.tsx` → the Book, Sandbox, and Agent windows.
- Problem: three portfolio proof surfaces present unsupported simulations or estimates as factual product evidence. `app/components/CaseStudySandboxApp.tsx` attributes GPS, CRDT, battery, latency, Lighthouse, and cold-start results to Trekky/Solynth and labels them “Verified Metrics”; `app/components/AgentWorkflowApp.tsx` calls an OpenRouter-backed request a “private model” on a “local model endpoint” and presents inferred UI phases as model/tool telemetry; `app/components/BookConsultApp.tsx` turns hard-coded day weights into delivery estimates and package durations.
- Design evidence: `PRODUCT.md` permits verified professional objects and original interactive experiments, but explicitly forbids invented benchmarks or commercial claims and requires every claim and artifact to remain truthful (`Capabilities and Constraints`, `Product Principles` 5). The only locally documented facts available for reuse are the operating context in `PRODUCT.md`: Sam operates Trekky.app, works through Solynth Labs, and has experience across the named product, interface, data, mobile, edge, and AI-workflow domains.
- Owner: copy and presentation in `app/components/CaseStudySandboxApp.tsx`, `app/components/AgentWorkflowApp.tsx`, `app/components/BookConsultApp.tsx`, and their registry summaries in `app/lib/workbench-system.ts`; provider-facing error copy in `app/api/ai/route.ts` reaches the Agent window.
- Scope and affected surfaces: Sandbox’s Trekky and Systems Architecture tabs, Agent’s header/badge/phase panel/completion copy, Book’s estimator/model cards/summary/generated brief, dock/search summaries for those apps, and Agent error states returned by `/api/ai`.
- Uncertainty: no repository evidence substantiates the attributed benchmarks, implementation details, engagement durations, or private/local-model claim. If the product owner supplies a dated primary source for an individual claim, that one claim may remain with its source and measurement context.

## Design decision

Treat these windows as interactive demonstrations, planning aids, or reference patterns—not as product telemetry, verified case-study results, provider internals, or quotes. Remove unsupported attribution and quantified performance/commercial claims. Where generated or simulated values remain useful, label them visibly as simulated in the surface that displays them, not only in hidden text or documentation.

## Reuse

- Verified positioning and capability vocabulary from `PRODUCT.md` under `Operating Context` and `Capabilities and Constraints`.
- The factual tone of the existing Now and Stack compositions in `app/components/WorkbenchOSV3.tsx`: current work, named tools, and operating status without outcome claims.
- Exemplar: the Motion Springs tab in `app/components/CaseStudySandboxApp.tsx`, which exposes an actual local interaction and its editable parameters without claiming production outcomes.

No new claim component is required. The existing app headers, tags, metric grids, sidebars, and summary panels can express the corrected framing.
